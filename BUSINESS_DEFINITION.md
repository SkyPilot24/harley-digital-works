## Business and customer

Harley Digital Works is the working customer-facing brand under The Harley Group. Provide a productised digital operating service for UK trades: a high-authority website, working enquiry desk, and ongoing digital care. Use templates and automation to eliminate administration. Do not promise rankings, leads or revenue.

Target niche: Established electrical contractors with roughly 2–8 staff, demonstrable trade accreditations (NICEIC/NAPIT), digital weaknesses, and capacity for high-value enquiries. Initial pilot focus: Bristol, Bath, and the M4 Corridor. Expansion into heating/plumbing follows pilot validation.

## Acquisition and delivery principles

No dependence on cold calling. Automate discovery, evidence-backed assessment, eligibility checks, scoring, draft outreach, permitted follow-ups and CRM administration. Use a corporate-business outreach lane with verified eligibility and a separate opt-in/inbound lane for sole traders and uncertain entity types. Public contact details alone do not establish permission. Do not send campaigns until controls and service fulfilment are verified and sending is authorised.

Prefer interest-first assessment; create private, clearly labelled previews for interested qualified prospects. Do not generate full speculative websites, fabricated reviews, credentials, photos or revenue estimates.

Journey: discovered → qualified → contacted → interested → meeting/demo → proposal → verified payment → onboarding → build → approval → launch → subscription care. A client workspace is not a won sale; a checkout return page is not payment proof.
