The identity

The selected number 3 Heritage Ring mark uses an H within two fine circles. It is a maker’s signature and group identity, never an independent certification. The double ring is retained in the primary mark; the single-ring screen icon is an intentional small-size adaptation.

Logo use

Use the primary endorsed logo where space allows. The compact version omits the provenance line for constrained placements; put the provenance elsewhere on the page where useful. Keep clear space of at least one H-stem width around the mark. Minimum recommended screen widths: primary 320px; compact 200px; symbol 48px. Use the supplied simplified icon below 48px. These are practical starting rules; check actual legibility.

Do not stretch, rotate or add shadows to the logo. Do not insert the headline into the ring, add “approved” text or combine it with third-party accreditation badges.

Colour & typography

Navy #152B42 is the primary. Ivory #F5F1E8 is the warm background. Blue #356DCC is a restrained accent; white #FFFFFF supports digital layouts. Use navy text on ivory, or ivory/white on navy. Prefer white over blue for blue buttons. Avoid small blue text on ivory until contrast is checked.

Segoe UI Bold for headings and the wordmark; Segoe UI Regular for body and provenance. Font files are not bundled. Main SVG assets use outlined lettering and do not need installed fonts. Editable-text originals are in 07-Editable-Text-SVGs and need Segoe UI installed. PNGs preserve the supplied appearance.

Stationery and editing

Card artwork is 85 × 55 mm at trim. A4 artwork follows the A4 ratio. SVG files provide scalable artwork; PNGs are screen/raster companions. These are RGB designs, without printer bleed, crop marks or CMYK conversion. Have your printer prepare a print-ready proof. Replace every bracketed placeholder with verified details; add applicable legal details before business use.

SVG files open in vector design tools and browsers. Editable-text typography may shift if Segoe UI is unavailable; outlined SVGs remain fixed. The website image is a static design concept, not an implemented website. No photos, video, paid generations, publication or site changes are included.
