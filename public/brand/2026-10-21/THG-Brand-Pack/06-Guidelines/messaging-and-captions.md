## Copy boundaries

Describe actual work and experiments accurately. Do not invent customer results, funding, partnerships, successful products, years in business, domain valuations or guarantees. Labs prototypes are not automatically launched products. THG parent branding does not establish subsidiaries or other legal structures. The domain portfolio does not need a new standalone identity. Revival Fellowship remains outside the commercial brand family.
