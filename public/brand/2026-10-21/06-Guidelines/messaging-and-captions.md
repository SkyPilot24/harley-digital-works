# Harley Digital Works — messaging

Primary headline: More leads. Less admin.
Provenance line: Part of The Harley Group
Service descriptor: Websites. Enquiry systems. Digital care.

Supporting copy: We look after your website and enquiry systems, so customers can reach you and you can focus on the job.

Audience: Established UK trade businesses. Initial commercial focus: electrical contractors. Emphasise useful systems, clear communication and ongoing care. Respect customers as experts in their trade.

## Suggested captions

1. Your website should help customers understand what you do and take the next step. Harley Digital Works brings websites, enquiry systems and ongoing digital care together, so you can focus on the job.
2. On the tools all day? Your digital side still needs looking after. We help trade businesses create a clearer route from website visitor to customer enquiry.
3. Less digital hassle starts with the basics: clear services, easy contact and a website that stays cared for. That's the focus at Harley Digital Works, part of The Harley Group.

## Use with care

“More leads. Less admin.” is a marketing ambition, not a measured customer outcome or guarantee. Do not add numerical lead promises, fabricated results, reviews or trade accreditations. Use “designed to help” when discussing expected outcomes. These concepts do not establish service availability or launch readiness.
