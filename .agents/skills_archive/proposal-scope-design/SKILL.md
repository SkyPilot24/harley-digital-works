# Proposal & Scope Design

## When to Use

- Preparing proposals for new or expanding client work
- Defining deliverables and boundaries clearly
- Preventing scope creep before engagement starts

## Outputs

- Service package structure by objective and value
- Scope inclusions, exclusions, and assumptions
- Delivery plan with milestones and success criteria

## Guidance

- Keep scope language specific and measurable
- Link deliverables to client outcomes, not activity volume
- Call out dependencies, approvals, and change-control triggers
