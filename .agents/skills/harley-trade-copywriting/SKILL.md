# Harley Trade Technical Copywriting Engine

## Overview

Generates authoritative, conversion-focused website copy for UK trade contractors, highlighting mandatory trade accreditations (Gas Safe, NICEIC, FMB, CHAS Elite) and local trust signals.

## Guidelines

- Write with punchy, high-trust phrasing.
- Lead with immediate clarity: "You run your business. We'll handle your website."
- Emphasise commercial ROI: landing £3,000+ boiler installations, EV commercial charging contracts, or commercial maintenance retainers.
