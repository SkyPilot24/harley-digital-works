# Trade Website Copywriting

## When to Use

- Writing copy for new client website builds (Launch, Growth, or Pro packages).
- Refactoring existing client copy that sounds too generic, technical, or academic.
- Drafting service pages, local landing pages, emergency call funnels, or quote forms for trades (e.g. electricians, plumbers, roofers, builders, landscapers, commercial cleaners).
- Extracting core service benefits from a 15-minute client kickoff call with zero client homework.

## Core Copywriting Principles for Trades

### 1. Plain English & Zero Tech Jargon

- Never use abstract words like "synergy", "paradigm", "cutting-edge digital transformation", or "generative workflow".
- Speak directly to customer pain points: speed, reliability, tidy work, fair prices, guarantees, and emergency response.

### 2. The 5-Point Trade Landing Page Formula

Every trade service page must answer:

1. **What is the exact service?** (e.g. "Commercial Solar Panel Installation & Battery Storage")
2. **Where do you do it?** (e.g. "Serving Manchester, Stockport, Cheshire & Greater Manchester")
3. **Why choose you over a rogue trader?** (e.g. "NICEIC Approved, MCS Certified, 10-Year Workmanship Guarantee, 150+ 5-Star Reviews")
4. **How fast can you respond?** (e.g. "Free Quotes within 24 Hours • Emergency Callouts within 60 Mins")
5. **What is the clear next action?** (e.g. "Call [Phone Number] Now" or "Get an Instant Online Estimate in 60 Seconds")

### 3. Headline Formulas for Trades

- **Outcome + Speed**: _"Boiler Broke Down? Gas Safe Heating Engineers at Your Door in Under 2 Hours."_
- **Done-For-You Commercial**: _"High-Efficiency Commercial Solar Installations That Slash Business Energy Bills by up to 70%."_
- **Reputation & Trust**: _"Manchester's Trusted Architectural Builders. Fixed Price Quotes & 10-Year Guarantees on Every Extension."_

## Deliverable Checklist

- [ ] Direct Click-to-Call and WhatsApp link prompts written into hero and section footers.
- [ ] Trust badges, accreditations, and insurance coverage highlighted prominently.
- [ ] FAQs answering real pricing, timeline, and booking objections.
- [ ] Meta title (< 60 chars) and meta description (< 155 chars) optimized for local search intent.
