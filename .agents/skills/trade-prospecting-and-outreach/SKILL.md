## Handling Common Trade Objections

### 1. "I get all my work from Checkatrade / Bark / MyBuilder / Facebook."

> **Response**: _"Checkatrade and lead sites are great to start with, but you are competing against 4 other trades for every enquiry, driving your prices down. A dedicated professional website lets you own the customer relationship, charge premium rates, and stop paying £30+ per shared lead."_

### 2. "I don't have time to write text or organize photos."

> **Response**: _"That is exactly why 140+ trades hire us. You do zero copywriting. We write all the service descriptions, curate high-definition trade imagery, and build the lead funnels. All we need is a 15-minute phone call to learn about your favorite jobs."_

### 3. "I tried building a site on Wix / GoDaddy and it didn't get any work."

> **Response**: _"DIY site builders provide templates, but they don't give you trade-specific copywriting, local SEO geo-tagging, or fast mobile conversion funnels. Our sites are engineered from the ground up to rank on Google in your local towns and turn visitors into WhatsApp enquiries."_

### 4. "What if I need changes after launch?"

> **Response**: _"Every website includes our £95/mo Managed Care Plan. Whenever you finish a new job, change your prices, or get a new accreditation badge, simply WhatsApp us the photo or text and our UK team updates it within 24 hours."_
