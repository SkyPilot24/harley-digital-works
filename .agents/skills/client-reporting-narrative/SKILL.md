# Client Reporting & Narrative

## When to Use
- Writing weekly or monthly client summaries
- Turning raw metrics into stakeholder-ready updates
- Defining next-step actions from current performance

## Outputs
- Executive summary with business outcomes
- Performance narrative by channel and objective
- Action plan with owners, priorities, and expected impact

## Guidance
- Lead with outcomes, then explain drivers
- Keep language clear and non-technical for stakeholders
- Tie every recommended next step to a measurable objective
