# SEO & Content Strategy

## When to Use
- Building keyword clusters and topical maps
- Defining content briefs and page intent
- Prioritizing on-page optimization opportunities

## Outputs
- Prioritized keyword clusters by intent and funnel stage
- Brief-ready page outlines with headings and search intent
- Action list for metadata, internal linking, and content gaps

## Guidance
- Focus on business relevance and ranking opportunity, not just volume
- Tie recommendations to measurable outcomes (traffic, leads, revenue)
- Align recommendations with THG strategic direction when context is available
