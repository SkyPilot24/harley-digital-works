# CRO / Landing Page Optimization

## When to Use
- Investigating funnel drop-offs
- Prioritizing page-level conversion experiments
- Improving offer clarity, trust signals, and form completion

## Outputs
- Friction and drop-off diagnosis by funnel step
- Prioritized experiment roadmap by impact and confidence
- Recommendations to improve conversion intent and completion

## Guidance
- Tie recommendations to the highest-friction steps first
- Favor testable hypotheses over broad redesigns
- Balance conversion gains with lead quality
