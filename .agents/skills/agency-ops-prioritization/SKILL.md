# Agency Ops & Prioritization

## When to Use
- Prioritizing work across multiple clients and teams
- Balancing short-term deliverables and strategic initiatives
- Sequencing initiatives by impact, effort, and dependency

## Outputs
- Prioritized work queue with explicit trade-offs
- Capacity-aware recommendations by team function
- Next-cycle focus plan tied to revenue and delivery outcomes

## Guidance
- Prioritize by business impact and execution feasibility
- Make dependencies and blockers visible early
- Reassess priorities regularly against current capacity
