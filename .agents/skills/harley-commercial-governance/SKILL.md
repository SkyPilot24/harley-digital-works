# Harley Commercial Governance & Operating Protocol

## Core Purpose
Enforces institutional standards, commercial clarity, and strict quality gates across all client engagements within The Harley Group ecosystem.

## Pricing Standard & Payment Milestones
- **14-Day Launch Plan**: £1,495 (50% upfront deposit: £747.50, balance on completion).
- **Growth Website Platform**: £2,995 (50% upfront deposit: £1,497.50, balance on completion).
- **Enterprise Digital Product**: £4,995+ bespoke.
- **Managed Care Desk**: £95/mo (Hosting, SSL, security, backups, updates).

## Key Deliverables & Operating Rules
1. **100% Done-For-You**: No homework or copywriting tasks required from the client.
2. **Zero Tech Jargon**: All client communications conducted in plain English.
3. **14-Day Delivery SLA**: Guaranteed progression from intake to live Google verification in 14 days.
