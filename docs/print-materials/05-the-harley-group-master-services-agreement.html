Operating Entities: Harley Digital Works · THG Digital
Master Services Agreement (MSA)
Ref: THG-MSA-2026-UK

1. Engagement & Scope of Delivery

1.1 Services: The Harley Group, operating through its specialised operating subsidiaries including Harley Digital Works and THG Digital (the "Company"), agrees to deliver website design, digital infrastructure, copywriting, managed hosting, and technical operations as specified in the agreed Order Form or Proposal Matrix.

1.2 14-Day Delivery Commitment: For standard Launch Website projects, the Company commits to completing development and presenting the pre-launch preview within fourteen (14) calendar days following receipt of deposit and onboarding information.

2. Intellectual Property & Ownership

2.1 Complete Client Ownership: Upon receipt of full and final payment for website build packages, all intellectual property rights in bespoke graphics, website copy, layouts, and brand assets developed specifically for the Client shall transfer 100% and unencumbered to the Client.

2.2 Proprietary Frameworks: General reusable toolkits, hosting configurations, CMS boilerplates, and deployment scripts remain the operational IP of The Harley Group, licensed to the Client on a perpetual, non-exclusive basis for the lifespan of the deployed website.

3. Managed Care & Service Level Agreements (SLA)

3.1 Monthly Care Plans: Managed care plans (£95/mo standard rate unless otherwise agreed) cover high-speed UK cloud hosting, continuous SSL certificate renewals, daily encrypted off-site backups, and up to 30 minutes of priority content/photo updates each calendar month.

3.2 Response Times: Critical incidents (website unavailability or form failure) receive a target response time under two (2) hours during UK business hours (08:30–18:00 Mon–Fri). Routine maintenance updates are implemented within one (1) working day.

3.3 Rolling Terms: Care plans operate on a flexible 30-day rolling term with zero multi-year lock-ins. Cancellation requires 30 days' written notice.

4. Commercial Terms & Payment Schedule

4.1 Turnkey Websites: Commercial website builds are invoiced on a 50% upfront commencement deposit and 50% upon final sign-off prior to public domain DNS cutover.

4.2 Taxes: All commercial rates quoted are in GBP and subject to UK VAT at the prevailing statutory rate.
