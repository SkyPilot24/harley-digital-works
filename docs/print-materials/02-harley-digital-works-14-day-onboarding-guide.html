How We Build & Launch Your Website in 14 Days

Our process is designed for busy business owners. We do all the heavy lifting, copywriting, and technical setup. Total time required from you: under 45 minutes.

Days 01–03
Step 01

15-Minute Discovery & Copywriting

We hold a 15-minute kickoff call to understand your target jobs, most profitable services, and local trade coverage. Our team writes all sales copy from scratch.

  • Your input: 15-minute phone chat + share your logo (if you have one).
  • Our delivery: Complete 5-to-10 page sales copy drafted with local keyword targeting.
Days 04–09
Step 02

Design & Mobile-First Engineering

We build your custom website on our ultra-fast architecture. We curate high-res trade photography, configure click-to-call buttons, and integrate Google Reviews.

  • Your input: None. You stay focused on your day-to-day business.
  • Our delivery: Fully functioning staging website, ready for private preview.
Days 10–12
Step 03

Private Preview & One-Round Tweaks

We send you a private link on your mobile and laptop. We review it together on a quick 20-minute call and apply any tweaks within 24 hours.

  • Your input: 20 minutes to review pages and give your final sign-off.
  • Our delivery: Any text or photo adjustments finalized immediately.
Days 13–14
Launch

Live Launch to Google & Managed Care Setup

We point your domain name, install SSL security, map your Google Business Profile, and activate daily cloud backups. Your new website is officially live to customers!

  • Ongoing: Your site transitions into our £95/mo Care Plan for 24/7 peace of mind.

Summary of What We Need From You

1. Logo & Contact Details: Email your logo, phone number, and primary email address.
2. Domain Access: Share your domain registrar login (or let us register a new domain for you).
3. Top 3 Services: Confirm the top jobs you want the phone to ring for.
4. Service Areas: List your main towns, cities, or postcodes.
