10 October 2026 · Founder decision report · H49 / D01–D08

## 1. Executive recommendation

**Choose Option B: operate domain sales through Harley Digital Works as a separately measured THG commercial workstream.** THG oversees the assets and commercial policy; HDW provides the customer-facing service, enquiries and administration. Keep legal ownership as currently documented until verified; a brand allocation does not transfer a domain or establish a separate company.

This confirms the 3 October founder direction and current BUSINESS_DEFINITION.md rather than reopening the brand architecture. Sell existing inventory first. No new brand, company, domain acquisitions, paid subscriptions or custom checkout is justified by the evidence today.

Domains offer occasional cash receipts and possible website work, but are not proven recurring revenue. Protect the first paid HDW trade pilot. Start with a proposed maximum two hours weekly on domains; reassess using collected contribution per founder hour. Harley Labs can test reminder/research automation using fictional data. It should not become the commercial owner or production dependency.
