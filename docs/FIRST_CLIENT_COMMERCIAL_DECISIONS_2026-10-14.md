## Tax wording: verified distinction, business status still unknown

HMRC separates VAT registration rules from the VAT rates applying to goods/services. Being below the registration threshold does not establish that a supply is zero-rated or that the seller is unregistered; voluntary registration is possible. Confirm the actual seller status and invoice treatment. This review makes no tax-setting change or determination of HDW's registration. [HMRC registration guidance](https://www.gov.uk/register-for-vat), [VAT rates](https://www.gov.uk/vat-rates).

Local customer-facing copy now refers to the written quote confirming any applicable taxes, rather than asserting either zero VAT or +VAT. ## Minimal evidence to attach to the first real agreement

- Approved seller fact sheet and tax/invoice instruction, with date/source.
- Final approved package, sold channels, support/backup/recovery/cancellation/ownership rules and spending boundaries.
- Customer/project ID and readable agreement revision/hash, authorised signatory, explicit acceptance/time and revision rules in the protected customer system.
- Secure intake: real services/areas, accreditation evidence, rights-cleared assets, domain/DNS/email authority and missing-input holds. No passwords or buyer/client personal data in shared documents or URLs.
- Provider-confirmed receipts linked to the approved agreement; exact preview revision, customer approval, QA/release/rollback evidence and monitored care owner.
- Actual acquisition/build/support minutes, fees and costs. The illustrative economics assume the full setup fee; a half-deposit is not the full setup margin or proof of instant payback.

These requirements should become reusable approved rules after the pilot, not a permanent founder approval step for every standard proposal. Keep customer approval and reviews of exceptional/nonstandard risk explicit.
