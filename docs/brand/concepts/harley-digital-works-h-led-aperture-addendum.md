## Addendum Goal

The original Open Aperture direction felt premium, calm, and memorable, but the symbol did not make enough use of the Harley name. This addendum keeps the same clarity-led visual world while exploring three H-centred marks.

The H should feel like a founder and family brand, not an automotive reference. It should be clear enough to recognise at small sizes, but restrained enough to avoid becoming a generic monogram.

## Shared Visual Foundation

All three directions keep the Open Aperture brand world:

- Central idea: clarity, focus, simplification, and a clear path through digital complexity.
- Palette family:
  - Aperture Black `#0B0F10`
  - Deep Green `#0E5F52`
  - Signal Coral `#D95D39`
  - Soft Paper `#FBF8F1`
  - Pale Field `#E7ECE6`
- Type direction:
  - Body/UI: Manrope
  - Display: restrained Fraunces or Newsreader usage where a premium editorial moment is useful
  - Operational labels: IBM Plex Mono or DM Mono
- Logo behaviour:
  - Must work in light, dark, single-colour, and small-size contexts.
  - Must avoid thin details that fail at favicon size.
  - Must avoid motorcycle, shield, wing, flame, gear, circuit, robot, browser-window, or generic AI cues.

## Direction 3A: H Aperture

### Strategic Concept

H Aperture is the closest continuation of Open Aperture. The mark uses open frame corners and internal spacing so the H becomes the clear centre of the symbol. It suggests Harley Digital Works as the place where scattered digital work is brought into focus.

Why it fits:

- Keeps the premium, clarity-led feel of Direction 3.
- Gives the logo a stronger Harley anchor without becoming overly decorative.
- Works well for favicon, app icon, social avatar, proposal covers, and website navigation.

Advantages:

- Strongest continuity from Direction 3.
- Most premium and restrained of the H-led options.
- Best candidate if the goal is a mark that feels established and calm.

Possible weaknesses:

- The H depends on careful spacing and refinement.
- If the final vector is too subtle, the H may read as a frame rather than a letter.

### Logo Concept

- Primary logo: open H aperture symbol plus Harley Digital Works wordmark.
- Wordmark: refined, calm, modern; Manrope or a customised wordmark derived from the final type direction.
- Symbol: open corner frame with a central H formed by solid aperture strokes and negative space.
- Horizontal arrangement: symbol left, wordmark right, optional parent-company line below.
- Stacked arrangement: symbol above wordmark, tagline optional below for presentation covers only.
- Small-size treatment: simplified symbol with fewer corner details and no tagline.
- Favicon concept: aperture black square with soft paper H and a coral focus point or path.
- Single-colour treatment: all black, all white, or all deep green.
- Reversed treatment: soft paper H on aperture black.

### Small-Size Notes

The final favicon should remove any secondary label, parent line, and fine internal cuts. The H must remain readable at 16x16 as a bold shape, with coral either removed or used as one simple dot/stroke only.

## Direction 3B: Harley Frame

### Strategic Concept

Harley Frame is warmer and more founder-led. The symbol is a composed architectural H: two vertical forms, a measured crossbar, and open corners that imply a frame rather than a corporate badge. It feels practical, built, and reliable.

Why it fits:

- Treats Harley as a family/founder name.
- Feels more human and service-led than a purely abstract symbol.
- Supports the agency side of the business: websites, care plans, support, and client relationships.

Advantages:

- Clearest H recognition.
- Warmest and most approachable of the three.
- Easy to reproduce in one colour.
- Strong for proposals, business cards, documents, and email headers.

Possible weaknesses:

- Slightly less distinctive than H Aperture.
- Needs refinement so it does not become a generic H monogram.

### Logo Concept

- Primary logo: architectural H symbol plus Harley Digital Works wordmark.
- Wordmark: confident, human, and clear; likely Manrope or a customised sans wordmark.
- Symbol: two vertical H pillars with an open crossbar/path, supported by aperture corner cues.
- Horizontal arrangement: symbol left, wordmark right, parent-company line optional.
- Stacked arrangement: symbol above wordmark for social and proposal covers.
- Small-size treatment: H pillars and crossbar only; remove outer corner cues below 32x32.
- Favicon concept: soft paper square with aperture black H and coral crossbar.
- Single-colour treatment: all aperture black or all soft paper.
- Reversed treatment: soft paper H on black with optional coral crossbar.

### Small-Size Notes

This is the safest option for readability. At favicon size, the mark can become a simple bold H with the crossbar kept open or coral, depending on contrast.

## Direction 3C: Signal H

### Strategic Concept

Signal H is the most digital and product-oriented of the addendum options. It keeps the H as the main form, then sends a single coral route through it to suggest progress, workflow, automation, and a clear path through systems.

Why it fits:

- Strongly supports SaaS, automation, digital products, and ContentFlow AI.
- Gives the identity a motion idea that can extend into product UI and launch graphics.
- Keeps the logo simple while making it more active than the other two options.

Advantages:

- Most scalable into motion, SaaS interfaces, diagrams, and product storytelling.
- The coral path gives memorability without generic AI styling.
- Works well across dark hero sections and app icons.

Possible weaknesses:

- Slightly more technical than Harley Frame.
- Coral must stay restrained so it does not become trendy or overactive.
- If the signal stroke becomes too thin, small-size clarity drops.

### Logo Concept

- Primary logo: refined H symbol plus Harley Digital Works wordmark, with a single coral route stroke.
- Wordmark: precise and modern; Manrope or a custom sans treatment.
- Symbol: sturdy H structure with one diagonal or stepped path through the centre.
- Horizontal arrangement: symbol left, wordmark right.
- Stacked arrangement: symbol above wordmark and tagline for campaign/social use.
- Small-size treatment: H and route only; remove frame corners and secondary detail.
- Favicon concept: aperture black square with soft paper H and coral route.
- Single-colour treatment: all black, all white, or all deep green; route becomes same colour.
- Reversed treatment: soft paper H on black with coral route.

### Small-Size Notes

The signal path should be at least as thick as the H stroke at favicon scale, or it should be removed in the 16x16 version. The H cannot depend on the coral route to remain readable.

## Recommendation

Recommended option for refinement: H Aperture.

Reason: it preserves what worked in Direction 3 while directly solving the missing H problem. It is premium, calm, and likely to make the strongest favicon and social avatar once refined.

Secondary option: Signal H if the brand should lean further into automation, SaaS products, and motion.

Safest readability option: Harley Frame if the priority is immediate H recognition and warm founder-led trust.
