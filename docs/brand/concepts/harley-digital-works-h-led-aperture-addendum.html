H-led Open Aperture logo directions

Three new symbol routes that keep the premium clarity-led world of Open Aperture while making the Harley H central to the logo system.

Review-only concepts. These marks are not final vector artwork and must not replace live logos, favicons, PWA icons, social previews, website branding, or dashboard branding before approval.

Direction 3A

H Aperture

The closest continuation of Open Aperture. The H sits inside an open frame, giving the mark a stronger Harley anchor while preserving the premium clarity-led character.

  • Best for continuity with Direction 3 and a premium favicon.
  • H is central, but still framed by the clarity and focus idea.
  • Needs careful spacing so the H remains clear at the smallest sizes.

Direction 3B

Harley Frame

A warmer, founder-led H built like a precise frame. It keeps the aperture language, but puts immediate H recognition and practical trust first.

  • Strongest H clarity and safest small-size performance.
  • Feels built, reliable, and personal without becoming informal.
  • Needs refinement so it does not feel like a generic letterform.

Direction 3C

Signal H

The most digital and product-oriented route. A sturdy H carries one coral signal path, suggesting progress, workflow, automation, and simpler systems.

  • Strongest for motion, SaaS interfaces, and automation storytelling.
  • More active and distinctive than Harley Frame.
  • Signal stroke must stay bold enough for small-size clarity.
