# Prepared Harley Digital Works assets — 13 October 2026

Repository subset of the prepared voice-chat brand pack. The founder approved the direction and refined mockups. Vector artwork reconstructs that concept with custom curved H and Arial-based outlined lettering; small visual differences remain. No trademark clearance is claimed.

- `logos/`: endorsed and compact wordmarks and H symbols, each in navy, ivory, black and white (12 SVGs).
- `brand-tokens.json`: palette and typography specifications.
- `Harley-Digital-Works-Brand-Guide.pdf`: supplied eight-page guide.

Main name: Harley Digital Works. Exact endorsement: **Part of The Harley Group**. Palette: midnight #081B30, ivory #F6F3ED, supporting blue #173C62, slate #526479.

Preserve proportions, spacing and endorsement. Keep clear space of at least one H stem width. Use compact/symbol variants where the endorsement becomes too small to read. SVG lettering is outlined and requires no font installation. Use navy on ivory or ivory on navy; check actual size and contrast before publication.

The full pack also includes PNG, social and editable template outputs; they are not duplicated here. Current website source now uses dated public copies of these assets; compatibility aliases, icons and social preview are updated. This folder remains the documentation copy.
