## Method and limits

Public search and linked business pages establish services and geography; business claims are labelled as claims. Companies House was checked for the five numbered records in the first ten. RG, Bath-Electrical, Nextgen and Holts have matching name/locality or corroborating directory evidence; Aztec's same-name record remains an unresolved operator match. Companies House shows Active for these five records, but incorporation is not proof of current trading, size, accreditation or permission to contact.

Read-only HTTP GETs returned 200 for all ten shortlisted homepages and corroborated the stated raw-content patterns; evidence is in [the retrieval record](evidence/prospect-public-pages-2026-10-11.json). This does not verify whether every text fragment is visually displayed. No browser screenshots, mobile layout, speed, schema completeness, form submissions, email delivery, SMS response or search ranking were tested. No leads-lost or revenue estimates are made.

Only company-level information is saved. No directors' personal details, customer records, prospect email addresses or phone numbers are copied into the shared repository. Public contact pages can be consulted privately when a recipient is approved.
