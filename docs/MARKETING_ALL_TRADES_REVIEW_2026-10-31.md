## Findings

- Homepage already addresses UK trades. Its design studies cover building/renovation, solar and bathrooms; six before/after choices cover plumbing/heating, electrical, roofing, building, landscaping and decorating. Keep these explicitly fictional labels. Electrical selection is the default in both before/after and the four-category trade showcase; footer categories cover only four specialisms. This is emphasis and discoverability friction, not evidence that the live form rejects other trades.
- Live /services is broadly worded and uses a solar design study. Its before/after defaults to electrical. It has no clear all-trades eligibility sentence.
- Live /contact is neutral and has a free-text help field, optional business name/phone/website and no electrical-only selector. Its review invitation assumes an existing website even though the page also welcomes new websites. No all-trades intake restriction was observed; storage/backend handling remains untested.
- Homepage says “PILOT CAPACITY: 2 SPOTS” as well as two active builds. This can confuse simultaneous capacity with the three-customer pilot total or unverified current availability.
- Service promises need a separate trust review: /services advertises a paid receptionist bundle with two-second answering and 30-second WhatsApp delivery. Homepage advertises daily backups, weekly automated enquiry checks, Enterprise hosting, an instant WhatsApp illustration and zero-risk deposit wording. These are observed claims, not verified capabilities. Older acceptance records leave receptionist rollout, sold channels and contract wording unresolved. Do not treat this review as accepting them.

## Service and marketing plan

Lead with one existing Managed website-and-care offer for any trade. Tailor copy, services, real project photos and relevant verified accreditations to each business; do not require NICEIC/Gas Safe or a particular headcount of unrelated trades. Keep domains and Labs as separate existing workstreams. Specialist calculators, automated routing and receptionist capabilities require their existing scoped fulfilment/acceptance evidence before inclusion in a proposal.

Prioritise inbound journeys and permitted, evidence-based prospect qualification. Assess offer fit, actual website/enquiry problems and available delivery capacity across trades. Do not enable campaigns, scrape new lists, create advertisements or contact prospects under this review. Bristol/Bath/M4 wording is unchanged by the decision; reconcile the existing UK-wide FAQ wording separately without inventing a geographic change.
